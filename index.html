About Us

We are a group of passionate college students dedicated to sharing the art of traditional Chinese Dragon and Lion Dance.

Dragon Dance and Lion Dance originated in ancient China more than 2,000 years ago, and both are now performed around the world. Founded in 2015, the Northeastern University Dragon and Lion Dance Troupe gives students the opportunity to learn, practice, and become part of these traditions, helping to share the culture and keep it alive for future generations.

We have grown to perform on campus and throughout the Greater Boston area for both public and private events, from cultural celebrations to weddings and more. Recent highlights include performing Dragon Dance for the Boston Celtics at TD Garden and Lion Dance at the Asian American Festival on Boston Common.
